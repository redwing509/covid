# Unit 2, first sub-unit — isolation and culture

**This is not medical advice.** Nothing here diagnoses, treats, or replaces care from a qualified clinician. This write-up is a literature check of opened sources on isolation and culture only. It is not a conclusion about hypothesis H-A or H-B, and it does not finish the rest of unit 2 (electron microscopy, transmission studies, animal challenge, or variant tracking). Database labels such as SARS-CoV-2 are used as labels found in the sources, not as a classification finding by this project.

**Status:** first sub-unit write-up, 24 Nov 2026, Pacific Time. Four independent laboratory isolation-or-culture papers were opened as full text and citation-checked. A published comment-and-reply exchange that discusses isolation, purification, and Koch’s postulates was also opened. This page reports what those opened sources said. It does not decide whether a circulating pathogen is the main cause of the illness.

## How this check was run

- Research jobs were run by Hermes on the box, not by the controller inventing findings, using the we-research model **NVIDIA NIM `nvidia/nemotron-3.5-super-vl-120b-a12b`** in bite-size jobs (James t1358u / t1337u). The strict rule was: link and quote every factual claim from a page opened in that job, or write UNKNOWN / NOT FOUND.
- After Hermes finished, the controller fetched the cited pages independently and checked the recorded quotes. Only quotes that appear on an opened page are used below. A spliced two-sentence quote is printed as two separate quotes. A paraphrase is not treated as a verbatim quote.
- Pass 1 sources are listed in [`SOURCES-OPENED.md`](SOURCES-OPENED.md). Machine-readable job outputs and citation-check notes are in [`data/`](data/). The file [`data/HERMES-ASSEMBLE-DRAFT.md`](data/HERMES-ASSEMBLE-DRAFT.md) is the model’s own assemble draft; this page is the checked write-up.
- No Sci-Hub or Library Genesis was used. Official journal HTML and, where needed, the journal’s own PDF download were used.

## Not found / not done

- This is only the first sub-unit of unit 2. Electron microscopy as a separate check, household and other transmission studies, animal challenge studies, and variant tracking were not searched in these jobs.
- Hermes did not find a peer-reviewed isolation-method dispute with a published reply other than the IJVTPR comment/reply pair above, after searches aimed at journal letters and replies. That is a search result from these jobs, not a proof that no other dispute exists.
- No quantitative titer numbers (for example pfu/ml) were copied from the isolation papers, because none appeared in the quotes that were checked.
- Test 1 (genome classification) is a separate unit. This page does not change the Test 1 letter.

## What this page does not claim

It does not claim that H-A is confirmed or that H-B is refuted. Independent laboratories, in the opened papers, described isolation or culture of a virus they labeled SARS-CoV-2 in Vero-cell systems, and one of those papers also reported failed first attempts from low-titre specimens. A later comment argued that a purified germ and a specified disease were not available for testing Koch’s postulates; the authors of the original review replied on that point. Those are the opened records for this sub-unit.
